About

A veteran-owned engineering firm

Koop Solutions is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business, certified by the U.S. Small Business Administration, specializing in advanced computing and enterprise IT modernization. Built on 20+ years of software development and mission-critical delivery, we help federal systems integrators translate complex technical requirements into fully operational capabilities.
